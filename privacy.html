Property Radar - Privacy Policy

Purpose

Property Radar is a personal-use application for managing property research and backups.

Google Drive Access

The application uses the Google Drive drive.file permission to create, read, update and manage files created by the application or explicitly made available to it.

Storage and Sharing

Data is stored locally on the user's computer and in their own Google Drive. The application does not sell user data or share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Data Control

Users retain control of their files and may delete them from their computer or Google Drive. Google account access can be revoked at any time through Google Account connections.
